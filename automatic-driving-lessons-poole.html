Learning in Poole

You learn on the roads you'll be tested on.

Most of Gillian's learners take their test at the Poole test centre. She gets you used to the roads around it well before the day, so nothing on the test feels new.

You start somewhere quiet. No busy roundabouts until you're ready. Then you build up, at your pace, to the junctions and traffic you'll meet on the day.

Every lesson is 90 minutes, one-to-one with Gillian, in a dual-control automatic.
