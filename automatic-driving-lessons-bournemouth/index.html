Bournemouth and nearby lesson areas

The advertised teaching area includes Bournemouth, Westbourne, Talbot and Wallisdown, alongside neighbouring Poole locations. Coverage and collection arrangements should be confirmed directly with Gillian, especially if you're on the edge of the area.

First-time learners

Never driven before? Your first drive isn't a test. You'll get familiar with the car and begin somewhere that suits your experience, with Gillian's guidance and dual controls there when needed.

Switching from manual

If the clutch and gears are draining your attention, moving to an automatic removes that part of the task. You still learn the essential road skills, but with fewer controls to manage.

Returning after a bad experience

Had a previous instructor who rushed you, or taken a long break? Gillian is used to helping people start again without judgement. Tell her what went wrong, so the next lesson is different.

Flexible way to begin

Lessons cost £80 for 90 minutes. There's no contract or block of lessons you must buy. You can start with one lesson and see whether Gillian's approach suits you.

Frequently asked questions

Can you teach me in Bournemouth rather than Poole?

Gillian advertises tuition across Bournemouth and Poole. Send her your postcode or pickup area by WhatsApp so she can confirm availability.

Are the lessons suitable for anxious learners?

Yes. Many of Gillian's reviews describe her patient approach with nervous learners and people whose previous lessons knocked their confidence.

Is an automatic licence different?

Yes. Passing your practical test in an automatic means you can drive automatic cars, but not manual cars unless you later pass a manual driving test.
