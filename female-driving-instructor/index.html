You know exactly who your instructor will be

Some people want a female driving instructor because they feel more relaxed asking questions. Others prefer someone experienced, patient and reassuring after lessons elsewhere went badly. With Auto Easy Driving School, you book directly with Gillian, not a call centre or a large school that assigns different instructors.

Gillian trained to teach in London before building her teaching experience across three decades. She holds the DVSA's highest instructor grade and teaches in a dual-control automatic car. That means she can help when needed while you build the skills to make decisions independently.

Her teaching style is calm, but she will still tell you clearly what to improve. You can ask for explanations, practise things more than once and take difficult roads in stages. Driving confidently takes practice. You don't need to arrive already confident.

Who Gillian teaches

Complete beginners

You'll start with the car's controls, clear explanations and a drive appropriate to your experience. You are not expected to know how to drive before your first lesson.

Nervous or anxious learners

If traffic, roundabouts or past experiences make driving feel intimidating, Gillian can break the process down into smaller, manageable steps. Read more about lessons for nervous drivers.

People returning after a break

Maybe you failed a test, felt rushed or stopped lessons years ago. You can explain what's happened so Gillian can assess where to begin again.

Learners switching from manual

Automatic lessons let you concentrate on steering, observation and road positioning without having to coordinate clutch control or gear changes.
