Learning around Poole

Gillian offers lessons in Poole and surrounding areas including Parkstone, Ashley Cross, Penn Hill, Branksome, Broadstone, Canford Cliffs and Sandbanks. If you're unsure whether your postcode is covered, ask Gillian directly before booking.

If you are completely new

Your first lesson starts with an introduction to the car and its controls, then driving somewhere appropriate to your experience. Gillian decides with you when you're ready for more demanding roads.

If you've taken lessons before

Tell Gillian where you stopped progressing, whether that's roundabouts, confidence, manual gears or an unsuccessful test. She can assess your driving and suggest what to work on next.

Getting ready for a test

Many local learners take their practical test at Poole test centre. Gillian helps learners prepare for the driving situations they are likely to face, not simply memorise a route.

Prefer an automatic?

There is no clutch or gear-changing to manage. You can focus on observation, steering, road positioning and making safe decisions. An automatic-only pass permits you to drive automatic vehicles.

Poole lesson questions

How much do automatic driving lessons in Poole cost?

Gillian charges £80 for a 90-minute one-to-one automatic lesson, as currently advertised. There are no upfront lesson packages required.

Do you cover my part of Poole?

The listed coverage includes Parkstone, Branksome, Broadstone, Canford Cliffs, Sandbanks and neighbouring areas. Message Gillian with your location so she can confirm pickup arrangements.

Do I need my theory test before my first lesson?

No. You need a valid provisional licence to start lessons, but you can learn to drive before passing your theory test. You must pass theory before booking a practical driving test.
